# Animal sound credits

Short real recordings for the "Hear it" buttons on the sign cards. Each plays only when tapped. Public
domain, CC0 or CC BY only; CC BY sounds are also credited on the site's /credits/ page.

## bee.mp3

- Source page: https://www.nps.gov/subjects/sound/sounds-bee.htm
  (file: https://www.nps.gov/nps-audiovideo/legacy/mp3/nri/avElement/nri-BeeYOSE.mp3)
- What it is: a bee buzzing, recorded in Yosemite National Park
- Author: U.S. National Park Service, Natural Sounds
- License: Public domain (work of a U.S. federal agency)
- Edit: the 2.9 s recording played twice to 5 s, faded in and out, level-matched, mono 64 kbps MP3
