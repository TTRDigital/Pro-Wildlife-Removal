Privacy Policy

Pro Wildlife Removal · Last updated November 11, 2026

This page explains what information Pro Wildlife Removal ("we", "us") collects when you visit our website or call us, and what we do with it. It applies to this website and to the phone number shown on it.

Who we are

Pro Wildlife Removal, 1324 Seven Springs Blvd., Suite 178, Trinity, FL 34655. Phone: (727) 855-6766.

What we collect

  • When you call us: your phone number, the date and time of the call, how long it lasted, and what you tell us so we can book and perform the work. Calls to the number on this website may be recorded and transcribed for quality, training and to keep an accurate record of the job. You will hear a notice at the start of the call.
  • When you visit this website: basic technical information such as the pages you view, your device and browser type, the approximate area you are in, and the ad or search that brought you here. We collect this with cookies and similar tools from Google (Google Tag Manager and Google Ads).

This website does not have a contact form, and we do not ask for payment details here.

How we use it

  • To answer your call, schedule an inspection, give you a quote and do the work.
  • To keep records of our jobs and follow up about your service.
  • To measure whether our advertising works and to improve our website and service.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with companies that help us run the business, such as our phone and call-recording provider, our customer-records and scheduling tools, and Google for advertising measurement. They may use it only to provide those services to us. We may also share it if the law requires.

Cookies and advertising

Cookies are small files your browser stores. We use them to measure visits and ad results. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and you can control how Google uses data for ads at adssettings.google.com.

How long we keep it

We keep call and job records for as long as we need them to serve you and to keep our business records, and then delete or anonymize them.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by calling (727) 855-6766 or writing to the address above. This website is not meant for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy we will post the new version on this page and update the date at the top.
