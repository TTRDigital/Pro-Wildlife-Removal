Signs You Have a Bat Problem

If you notice any of these, don't wait — bats never leave on their own.

Scratching, squeaking, or chirping in walls or attic at night

Guano (bat droppings) in the attic, on walls, or floors

Stains and grease marks near roosting areas

Bats flying in and out of your home at night

Our Complete Bat Removal Solution

01

One-Way Valve Removal

We install one-way exclusion valves over every active entry point. Bats exit on their own at night to feed — but the valve prevents them from re-entering. No trapping, no handling, completely humane.

02

Exclusion (Seal-Up)

Once all bats have exited through the valves, we remove the devices and permanently seal every entry point with bat-proof materials — so they can never return.
