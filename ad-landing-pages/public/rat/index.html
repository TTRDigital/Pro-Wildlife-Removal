Signs You Have a Rat Problem

If you notice any of these, don't wait — rats never leave on their own.

Scratching in walls or ceilings at night

Droppings in the attic, pantry, or garage

Gnawed wires, wood, or food packaging

Grease rub marks along walls and beams

Nests of shredded insulation or paper

A musty, ammonia-like smell
